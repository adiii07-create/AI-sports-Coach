==================================================

This is the first complete AI module.

Analyze:

B. MOVEMENT

- total movement distance
- left foot movement

- movement direction
- approximate movement speed

C. KNEE POSITION

Calculate knee angles using:

HIP → KNEE → ANKLE

Calculate:

- average knee angle
- minimum knee angle
- maximum knee angle

D. BALANCE

Create an explainable balance metric using:

- shoulders
- hips
- feet

E. RECOVERY

Estimate how effectively the player returns toward the center after moving away.

Keep this first implementation simple and explainable.

Do NOT claim professional biomechanical accuracy.

11. COACHING FEEDBACK
==================================================

Feedback must be based on the actual calculated metrics.

Example:

If recovery is weak:

"Your recovery movement appears slower than expected after reaching the movement point."

Suggestion:

"Focus on returning toward the center immediately after reaching the movement position."

If balance is weak:

"Your body position becomes less stable during movement."

Suggestion:

"Focus on maintaining control through your hips and knees while changing direction."

If knee position is weak:

"Your knee position changes significantly during the drill."

Suggestion:

"Practice maintaining a controlled knee bend throughout the movement."

Do not randomly select feedback.
